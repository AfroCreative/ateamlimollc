Limo Rental FAQ

Everything you need to know about booking a limo in Columbus. Still have questions? Call (614) 555-0142.

How much does a limo cost in Columbus, Ohio?

Limo pricing depends on the vehicle, date, number of hours and itinerary. Weekend nights and prom/wedding season cost more than weekdays. Request a free quote and we'll give you an exact, all-inclusive price with no hidden fees.

What areas do you serve?

We serve all of Columbus and Central Ohio, including Dublin, Westerville, Powell, Hilliard, Grove City, Gahanna, New Albany, Upper Arlington, Worthington, Delaware, Lewis Center, Reynoldsburg, Pickerington, Grandview Heights and Bexley. Long-distance trips are available on request.

Are you available 24/7?

Yes. We take reservations and run trips 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays and early-morning airport runs.

How far in advance should I book?

For weddings and prom, book 2–6 months ahead. For airport and corporate trips, 24–48 hours is usually enough, but we'll always try to accommodate last-minute requests.

Are your chauffeurs licensed and insured?

Yes. Our chauffeurs are professionally trained, licensed and background-checked, and our vehicles are fully insured commercial vehicles.

Is gratuity included?

We'll explain exactly what's included in your quote, gratuity included, so there are no surprises at the end of the night.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellation terms depend on the type of booking and how far in advance you cancel. We'll explain the policy clearly before you confirm your reservation.

Can I bring alcohol in the limo?

Passengers 21 and over may bring beverages on most adult bookings in accordance with Ohio law. Alcohol is never permitted on prom, homecoming or other student trips.
