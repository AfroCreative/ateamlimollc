About A Team Limo LLC

A locally owned Columbus limo company built on one idea: every client deserves A-Team treatment.

Who We Are

A Team Limo LLC is a Columbus, Ohio limousine and chauffeured transportation company serving all of Central Ohio. We started with a simple goal: give Columbus a limo service that shows up on time, communicates clearly, charges fairly and makes every ride feel special.

Today we drive couples on their wedding day, students on prom night, executives to meetings, travelers to CMH and friends out on the town, and we bring the same care and professionalism to every trip.

Our Promise

  • Punctuality: we arrive early, every time.
  • Safety: licensed, insured, background-checked chauffeurs and well-maintained vehicles.
  • Transparency: all-inclusive quotes with no hidden fees.
  • Hospitality: courteous chauffeurs who treat you like a VIP.

Proudly Local

We live and work in Central Ohio. We know the shortcuts on game day, the best photo spots for wedding parties, and the fastest way to the airport at rush hour. When you book with A Team Limo, you're supporting a local Columbus business.
